Recruitment period: Feb 25 – Mar 10, 2026

Requirements: You can apply by filling out an online form until Mar 10. If you have any questions or issues, feel free to reach out to us on our social media channels or via email!

  • Info Night I: Mar 5, 2026

What kind of people are you looking for?

Reliable, creative, confident, empathetic, resourceful, and motivated individuals

Why should you choose our organization?

At HÖK, you will surely find activities that match your interests and inspire you: whether it's representing student interests, integrating international students, evaluating scholarship applications, or supporting student organizations and colleges. You can also take on responsibility in shaping the internal operations of a large organization, gain insight into financial processes, organize exciting events that engage many students, or even manage HÖK's social media channels.
